## What the company does (evidenced)

Grand Empire HK is an apparel **stocklot / surplus trading** business.
The model: source garment stock (overstock, cancelled orders, factory
surplus, fresh production) from suppliers — mostly Chinese factories —
and sell it to retail buyers, mainly in South Africa and Oceania. Margin
is made on the spread between the sourced price and the sell price, per
piece, at volume.

## The flow of a deal (evidenced)

1. **Offer in / source out** — a supplier sends a stock offer (item, qty,
   fabric, price, photos), OR a buyer requests a type of product and
   Sohan hunts stock to fill it.
2. **Quote** — present the offer to a buyer with a per-piece price and MOQ.
   Deals carry a ref (e.g. DIS-80513-LLJ, GBT26-3334).
3. **Negotiate** — buyer counters on price/qty; often a small **test
   order** first (e.g. 1000u) before the full lot.
4. **Sample** — physical sample sent/approved before bulk.
5. **Confirm & raise order** — buyer confirms, internal team raises the
   order.
6. **Payment & ship** — accounts handles payment; merchandise handles
   shipping.
Terms that recur: **power packing**, MOQ, per-piece USD pricing, CADs,
lookbooks, "raise the order".

## How Winston makes money here (the job)

- Keep every live deal moving (see deals.md) — the #1 lever is speed:
  a quote that sits gets undercut or forgotten.
- Chase pending replies before deals go cold.
- Frame every decision in dollars (gap × qty = real money).
- Spot the big-volume / high-margin deals and make sure they get priority
  attention, not buried under small ones.
- Learn each buyer's patterns (what they buy, target prices, how they
  negotiate) and record it in their people/ file.
