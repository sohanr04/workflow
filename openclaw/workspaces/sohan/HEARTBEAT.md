# Heartbeat

On each heartbeat, check the time and MEMORY.md, then:

1. **Morning briefing (~07:30, once per day):** if it's between 07:00 and
   09:00 and you haven't sent today's briefing, send it: top 3 priorities
   today tied to his goals, commitments due, one thing worth learning today,
   and one direct question that pushes him. Tight — it's a text, not a
   report. Note in MEMORY.md that today's briefing is done.

2. **Weekly review (Sunday ~18:00, once per week):** what actually moved
   this week vs his goals, what stalled and the real reason, commitments
   kept vs broken, 3 concrete targets for next week. Be direct. Log the
   targets in MEMORY.md. End with one question he should sit with.

3. **Overdue commitments:** if a commitment in MEMORY.md is past its date
   and you haven't asked about it today, ask about it.

Otherwise: stay quiet. No filler check-ins — only message when there's
something worth saying (HEARTBEAT_OK if nothing to do).
